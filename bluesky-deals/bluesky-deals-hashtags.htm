About Sky Shop

We help you discover the latest product deals and items shared on Bluesky, all organized in a consistent and easy-to-browse format.

Anyone can participate by posting products using #skyshop. Influencers, creators, and affiliates are welcome and encouraged to share deals using the format below: 

#skyshop #*category

Item: *name of item, keep it short
Price: *price with $ sign
Brand: *brand name
Shop: *website name, Amazon, Walmart, etc
Visit: *short URL (only from marketplace websites)

*images can be included. only one category per post.

How it works

  • All feeds are specifically from #skyshop hashtags
  • We filter for posts containing specific product metadata like Item, Price, and Brand.
  • Use the filters on the right to narrow down your search by website or specific brands.

*This is a public tool and does not require a login to browse public feeds. *Some items may be affiliate ads from creators. We don't track you or sell your shopping data.
